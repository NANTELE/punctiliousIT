The problem

Business problems are often treated as technology problems too quickly.

A requirement is unclear. A process behaves differently than expected. Teams disagree about what should happen. Business rules become difficult to manage. A system produces results nobody fully understands. Important decisions depend on incomplete or inconsistent information.

Adding more software does not necessarily solve these problems.

Sometimes the first requirement is simply to understand what is actually happening.

How PunctiliousIT can help

Practical analysis focused on understanding the problem, clarifying the decision, and defining what the business actually needs.

Business Analysis

Clarify requirements, business rules, processes, constraints, dependencies and expected system behaviour.

Decision Analysis

Clarify the decision, available options, criteria, assumptions, constraints, risks, consequences and trade-offs.

Focused Analysis Services

Small, fixed-scope reviews of a particular requirement, decision, problem, business rule, process or system behaviour.

A different starting point

PunctiliousIT does not begin by assuming that the requested feature, technology or proposed solution is the answer. The analysis starts underneath the request.

What problem are we actually solving?
What must be true?
What decisions need to be made?
What rules and constraints govern the situation?
What assumptions are we making?
What happens if we are wrong?
The devil is in the details.
